Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

BerryStudio is a browser-based pattern-drafting and 3D fit tool. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. Signing in is optional — most features work fully without an account.

Data that stays on your device

Your pattern projects, measurements, drafting settings, and any AI provider API key you add yourself are stored in your browser's local/session storage. None of that is sent to our servers. An API key you choose to save persistently is encrypted first, with a passphrase only you know, and is used solely to call that provider directly from your own browser.

If you create an account

Account sign-in is handled by Supabase, our authentication provider:

  • Email/password — your email and a securely hashed password are stored by Supabase. We never see or store your plaintext password.
  • Continue with Google / Continue with Facebook — we receive your email address and account ID from that provider through a standard OAuth sign-in. We never see or receive your Google or Facebook password.
  • We keep one small record per account: subscription status (trial or active) and the date your trial started, used only to determine feature access.

Third parties involved

  • Supabase — hosts authentication and the account record described above.
  • Google / Meta (Facebook) — only if you choose to sign in with one of those; they authenticate you and share your email/account ID with us as part of that sign-in.
  • AI providers (e.g. OpenAI) — only if you add your own API key for an AI feature. Those requests go directly from your browser to that provider using your key; we never see or store that traffic.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no ad-tracking or analytics cookies.
  • No selling or sharing your data with data brokers.
  • No access to your pattern files or measurements — those never leave your browser unless you explicitly export or sync them yourself.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time to end your session.
  • To delete your account and its stored data, email us at the address below.
  • Clear all locally stored data (patterns, saved keys) any time via your browser's site-data settings.
